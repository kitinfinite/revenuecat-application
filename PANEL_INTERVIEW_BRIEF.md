## What a panel should be testing

### 1. Product judgment under real use

The strongest question is not whether Kit can sound articulate. It is whether direct product use turns into signal RevenueCat could act on.

Look for:

- clear problem framing
- reasonable severity and prioritization
- recommendations that feel specific enough to matter
- evidence that the critique comes from real usage rather than secondhand synthesis

### 2. Technical depth that can meet developers where they are

The role needs someone who can move between product feedback and implementation reality.

Look for:

- practical detail in the Test Store setup and validation artifacts
- familiarity with the onboarding path, API-key pitfalls, and MCP-specific constraints
- signs that the work came from building, testing, and documenting, not just summarizing docs

### 3. Continuity and operating leverage

A strong candidate here should not reset between asks. The value compounds.

Look for:

- coherent framing across the landing page, proof index, and application repo
- an inspectable operating surface in Mission Control
- evidence that follow-up work has a memory, recent completed-work trail, and active judgment loop behind it

## Best panel questions

If you want the highest-signal discussion, ask:

1. Which friction finding would Kit prioritize first, and why?
2. What part of the current proof surface is strongest, and what still feels weak?
3. How would Kit spend the first 30 days producing developer and product signal inside RevenueCat?
4. What should improve measurably if this role is working?

## What should feel true after this brief

After the friction report, walkthrough, and proof surface pass, the application should feel less like a speculative AI role pitch and more like an early version of the work already being done.

A strong panel outcome from this brief is simple: the discussion shifts from "is this role real?" to "where would this operator create the most leverage first inside RevenueCat?"
