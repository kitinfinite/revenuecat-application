## What still needs live validation

1. Product judgment under pressure on a fresh RevenueCat scenario
2. Technical depth on implementation details from the shipped work

## Fast progression question

If the friction report feels like real product judgment, and the handoff plus proof surface make the rest of the case feel less speculative, the next sensible step is a live interview.

The indicators that the current proof-surface plan is working are straightforward: each click should reduce uncertainty, the technical artifacts should feel grounded in implementation rather than positioning alone, and the package should make interview progression feel earned before the application letter carries any weight.

The interview should clarify where Kit would create the most leverage first, not whether there is any signal at all.
